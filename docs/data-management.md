# Managing business data

Super administrators manage data directly in each module. Select the company in the top bar, open the relevant module, and use **New** in its toolbar or **View**, **Edit**, and **Delete** beside an existing row or card. Editors and confirmation dialogs stay on that module's route. Saved changes refresh its lists, detail drawers, counts, and related views.

The workspace covers 68 record types across Corporate and Group, CRM, Sales, Contracts, Finance, HR, Legal, Procurement, Assets, Knowledge, Communication, Workflows, BI, and reference data. Documents retain their folder, upload, preview, move, title, and delete controls in the Documents module.

Use these controls to replace sample records with real information. Relationship fields show names and support search. Required fields are marked; optional fields can be cleared. Business document numbers are generated by the server. Invoice and procurement line totals are calculated from their quantities and prices.

The existing module forms still handle creation, allocations, approvals, and specialized setup. Contacts and activities have controls within the CRM partner drawer; contract milestones, obligations, variations, and claims have controls within the contract drawer. Proposal cards retain their native sales workflow and gain maintenance actions. Payments use **Reverse payment** in the Payments list.

Additional entities are accessible under **Related records** within their owning module. For example, HR includes departments, positions, skills, training, and certificates; Payments includes bank accounts; Procurement includes orders and supplier evaluations; BI includes dashboards and report definitions; Administration includes user groups and reference data. Choosing one opens its searchable CRUD table on the same module page.

Deletion checks linked records before making changes. Remove or reassign dependent records first, or deactivate a record with an Active setting. Organization records are deleted individually. Unused records with an archive column are archived. **Deleted records** in the module opens its archived records, where **Restore** brings them back. Restore preserves the inactive setting, and archived child records can be restored separately after their parent. Reference and child records without an archive column are permanently deleted; the confirmation states this explicitly.

Invoices can be edited and removed while in draft. Contracts, proposals, leave, and other workflow records retain their state restrictions; use their module to cancel, amend, approve, or change state. A recorded payment is reversed with an opposite bank movement and negative invoice allocations. Original movements remain in the ledger, and a payment cannot be reversed twice. Bank opening balances and currency are protected once movements exist; tax rates used on invoices must be replaced with a new tax code.

Existing data is never cleared automatically. For an empty company workspace, create the real company in Group and select it before entering its business records. Shared reference records remain available.

An administrator without any companies sees **Your workspace is empty** and a **Set up my company** link to Group. Creating the first company makes it available in the company selector and unlocks its modules. The normal `pnpm db:seed` initializes reference data and admin access without adding demo business records; sample data requires the explicit `prisma:seed:demo` command.

For a deliberately requested full reset, `pnpm --filter @octa-bms/api data:reset:preview` reports the records affected. Running `pnpm --filter @octa-bms/api exec tsx prisma/clear-business-data.ts --execute` first creates a PostgreSQL dump and copies uploaded files under `backups/workspace-reset-<timestamp>`, then removes all business records, companies, other users, and local uploads. Active super-admin accounts, their login sessions, built-in permissions, and essential reference lists are preserved. The backup includes `manifest.json`, `database.dump`, upload copies, and `result.json`. Restoring the dump with PostgreSQL `pg_restore` and copying uploads back to the manifest's original paths restores the pre-reset workspace.

## Implementation and verification

`apps/api/src/modules/records/record-catalog.ts` is the allowlist. Prisma metadata supplies field and relationship descriptions, while the catalog defines permitted fields, company scope, owned child records, workflow restrictions, native creation routes, and generated numbering. The service validates fields and references, rejects protected state changes, enforces system administrator access on every endpoint, and applies bulk mutations in a serializable transaction. Authentication secrets are excluded from responses. Cached permissions and the search index are refreshed after relevant changes.

`RecordActions`, `RecordEditor`, and `ModuleRecords` supply shared dialogs and module-local tables. `ListPage` maps primary list endpoints to their record resources; `app/module-records.ts` assigns related data to module routes. The old `/admin/data` route remains available for bookmarked links, while its sidebar entry and cross-module management shortcut have been removed.

The API lives at `/api/v1/records`. CRUD bodies use `{ "data": { ... } }`; bulk removal uses `{ "ids": [ ... ] }`. Other routes include `/catalog`, `/:resource/lookup/:field`, `/:resource/archived/list`, and `/:resource/:id/restore`.

Run unit tests and browser checks with:

```powershell
pnpm --filter @octa-bms/api test
pnpm --filter @octa-bms/web test
$env:OCTA_TEST_BROWSER_CHANNEL = 'chrome'
pnpm --filter @octa-bms/web test:e2e
```

The PostgreSQL integration check exercises every catalog, relationship lookup, schema-based creation, full form update, deletion, native-created records, and balanced payment reversal:

```powershell
pnpm --filter @octa-bms/api test:records:postgres
```

It uses the configured database, creates randomly named temporary records in a single transaction, and deliberately rolls the transaction back. It asserts that the temporary user did not remain. Existing business records are read for shared lookup values and are never mutated by the check.
